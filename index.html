📘 How Break-Even ROAS Works (Formulas & Logic)

1. The Core Break-Even Formula

Break-Even ROAS represents the return-on-ad-spend multiplier where you generate zero profit and zero loss. The mathematical relationship is:

Break-Even ROAS = Selling Price / (Selling Price - Product Cost - Shipping Cost)

2. Why Most E-Commerce Beginners Miscalculate

Many media buyers look only at blended ROAS reported in Meta Ads Manager. If your break-even ROAS is 2.5x and your campaigns report 2.1x, every additional sale is actively depleting your operating cash flow. Factoring in shipping and land costs first is mandatory.

3. Scaling Unit Economics Beyond Paid Traffic

When scaling DTC stores, gateway processing fees (e.g. 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction on Stripe or PayPal) and currency conversion loss can silently erode 3% to 5% of gross revenue. Learn how to configure compliant multi-currency routing in our Cross-Border Payment Gateway Comparison Hub.
